# Research v2: rule set with legal-text links

Researcher pass, 28 Oct 2026. Portal Legislativ pages were fetched and their titles checked; article numbers are from the fetched text unless marked "not confirmed". Figures marked press-only were not read in the act.

## Summary

1. Legea 141/2025 (MO 699, 25 Jul 2025): https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/300022 (fetched). VAT 21%/11%, dividends 16%, pension CASS all in art. II.
2. Electricity cap: OUG 6/2025 (MO 182, 28 Feb 2025) covered 1 Apr–30 Jun 2025; ended 1 Jul 2025 (press/ANRE).
3. Gas: OUG 12/2026 (MO 178, 9 Mar 2026) is adopted and in force 1 Apr 2026–31 Mar 2027. The text has no 0.31 lei/kWh ceiling; it uses a formula (15 lei/MWh supply cap, 110 lei/MWh producer price). Amended by OUG 19/2026 (MO 237, 26 Mar 2026).
4. Pilon II: Legea 2/2026 (MO 2, 5 Jan 2026) confirmed; art. 113: in force one year after publication (5 Jan 2027); art. 55: max 30% lump sum. 8-year schedule and 1,251 lei minimum are press-only.
5. Micro: OUG 89/2025 (MO 1203, 24 Dec 2025): 1% from 1 Jan 2026. The 100,000 EUR ceiling appears only in the preamble; OUG 8/2026 and the one-employee condition not verified.
6. IT / construction / agriculture facilities removed: OUG 156/2024 (MO 1334, 31 Dec 2024); fetched text truncated, article not confirmed. IT impact for 12,000 lei gross: about 650 lei/month more tax (researcher arithmetic: CAS 25%, CASS 10%, no personal deductions).
7. Building tax: Legea 239/2025 (MO 1160, 15 Dec 2025) confirmed; the 2026 increase for individuals is press-only (1,000 → 2,677 lei/m² taxable value, ~80% average). OUG 78/2025 (MO 1172) postpones some points to 1 Jan 2027; conflicting reports.
8. Minimum wage: HG 146/2026, 4,325 lei gross from 1 Jul 2026 (press only; MO number conflicts; not on the portal).
9. Recent window 1 Sep–28 Oct 2026: none found (MF, ANAF, MO news searched; no dated act or draft).
10. OECD/INFE 2023: press reports Romania 35th of 39; exact score not found (OECD page returned 403).
11. Revolut Romania: 5 million retail clients reported 30 Sep 2026; 4.8 million at end 2025.
12. Not verified: Pilon III art. 78 text (Codul fiscal page truncated), OUG 8/2026, HoReCa-specific 2026 changes.
